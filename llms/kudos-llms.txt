# Kudos

> Kudos (Kudos Technologies, Inc.) is a consumer credit-card rewards and smart-shopping
> assistant founded in 2021 by Tikue Anazodo and Ahmad Ismail. Its browser extension and
> mobile apps sit at online checkout and recommend which of a shopper's connected cards to
> use to maximize points, cash back, and card benefits. Kudos is backed by QED Investors.

Kudos publishes **no public developer API, developer portal, OpenAPI specification, or SDKs**
as of 2026-07-19. This file is generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the
company's public web surface; it is not a provider-published llms.txt.
